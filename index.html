01 / About A note on the work
Bio

I'm a Research Scientist at Adobe Research, where I am a key researcher in the post-training stack for small language models and vision-language models. My team's models serve grounding and retrieval use-cases across Adobe products. I also contribute to continuous image-editing models — see SliderEdit.

I completed my PhD at the University of Maryland with Soheil Feizi in the Center for Machine Learning. Before that, I spent two years at Microsoft AI as an Applied Scientist, with research stints at Microsoft Research (Cambridge & Redmond) and prior internships at Adobe.

My research sits at the intersection of understanding and control: how knowledge is stored and transferred inside multimodal models, and how to steer or edit those models with minimal intervention. Recent work spans mechanistic interpretability, post-training for VLMs, and large-scale reinforcement learning.

02 / Product Impact From papers to products

Research that ships. Below are models I've post-trained and taken to production at Adobe, across creative, marketing and enterprise surfaces.

Adobe Firefly · Creative

Continuous image editing, on a slider

Shipped post-trained continuous image-editing models to Firefly.com as the Tune / Generative Restyle feature. Instead of re-prompting, a user dials an edit's intensity up or down along a smooth trajectory, building on the research in SliderEdit (CVPR 2026 Oral) and SliderEdit++.

Adobe Brand Concierge · Enterprise

On-device attribution for grounded answers

Shipped on-device attribution models for Brand Concierge, Adobe's conversational AI for brand websites. Each generated sentence is traced back to the approved brand content it came from, so answers stay grounded and checkable. It builds on my work on post-hoc attribution (Decomposition-Enhanced Training, EACL 2026) and mechanistic circuits for extractive QA (COLM 2025).

Adobe GenStudio · Enterprise

State-of-the-art document parsing

Shipped state-of-the-art post-trained document-parsing models for enterprise use cases in GenStudio. Marketers upload brand guidelines and briefs as PDFs, and the VLM turns layouts, tables and figures into structured data that drives on-brand content generation.
